# Adversarial Review Reference

Use this before a contested filing is treated as final.

## Paid-counsel pass
- strongest viable theory;
- cleanest procedural vehicle;
- best evidence;
- narrowest sufficient relief;
- alternative relief;
- preservation strategy.

## Opposing-counsel / prosecutor pass
Attack:
- jurisdiction;
- standing/capacity;
- timeliness;
- service/notice;
- waiver/preservation;
- admissibility and authentication;
- factual gaps;
- causation/materiality;
- authority hierarchy;
- adequate alternative remedies;
- overbroad or non-signable relief.

## Trial-judge pass
Ask:
- what can be decided now?
- what facts are actually established?
- what is disputed?
- what standard applies?
- what legal authority permits each requested directive?
- what narrower ruling resolves the matter?
- what finding would an appellate court need in the record?

## Appellate-judge pass
Ask:
- is the issue preserved?
- is the order appealable?
- what standard of review controls?
- is the record complete?
- did the lower court make required findings?
- is any error harmless?
- does an alternative ground support affirmance?

## Scholar / legislative-drafter pass
Read text for:
- definitions;
- mandatory versus discretionary verbs;
- cross-references;
- exceptions;
- conditions precedent;
- remedial provisions;
- amendment history;
- structural consistency;
- constitutional limits.

## Repair rule

Do not merely list vulnerabilities. Repair each curable vulnerability in the filing, supporting record, requested relief or service plan. Clearly mark incurable or unresolved issues.

# Legal Work Matrix

Use one row per claim, defense, issue or requested directive.

| Issue / requested relief | Governing authority | Elements / standard | Supporting facts + provenance | Evidence / exhibit | Contrary authority / opposition | Response | Procedural vehicle | Service / deadline | Exact relief |

## Required closing checks

- Procedural posture verified
- Controlling authority verified
- Contrary authority searched
- Fatal defects scanned
- Timeline/deadlines verified
- Service matrix complete
- Evidence gaps marked
- Appellate preservation checked
- Citations and quotations verified
- Proposed order signable as written
